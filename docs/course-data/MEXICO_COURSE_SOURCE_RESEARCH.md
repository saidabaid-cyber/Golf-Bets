## Source rules for Course Master

1. `BackyardVerifiedProvider`: ingest only records already owned, contractually supplied, or manually verified with recorded provenance.
2. `USGAProvider`: disabled until USGA/FMG supplies express commercial authorization and a supported delivery contract.
3. `FMGProvider`: disabled until FMG supplies a licensed feed/export or authorizes another channel.
4. `ClubOfficialProvider`: enabled per club only after written permission and a source/effective-date record; no crawling.
5. `FutureLicensedProvider`: requires an executed commercial license, provider schema mapping and a Mexico coverage acceptance test.
6. Open-data sources must retain their own attribution and share-alike boundary; they cannot silently relicense provider-owned ratings.

## Recommended acquisition order

1. Send the USGA request after legal/product review and ask USGA who controls the Mexico commercial grant.
2. Open a parallel FMG institutional request for the official Mexican facility/course/layout/tee catalog and its update cycle.
3. Offer Mexican clubs a standard opt-in data agreement and verified scorecard submission path.
4. Run a paid-provider sample evaluation against a predeclared list of Mexican courses; compare completeness, freshness, stable IDs and rating provenance before contracting.
5. Use OSM or a licensed geocoder only to supplement location/aliases, never to infer rating, slope, par or scorecards.

No candidate in this review justifies claiming “Mexico complete.”
